Privacy Policy

Age of Cats (Era Gatuna) · Last updated: November 24, 2026

Age of Cats is a mobile game developed by Plenstudio and Buski87 ("we"). This policy explains what data is used when you play. If you have any questions, write to buski1987@gmail.com.

1. Data we store

The game does not ask you to sign up and does not create an account. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data. Your progress (cats, coins, settings) is saved only on your device; we do not send it to any server of ours. If you uninstall the game or use "Delete game" in Settings, that progress is removed.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads (for example, to get a boost) through Google AdMob. To do this, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process, depending on your settings and consent:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language),
  • ad interaction data (views, clicks) and performance or diagnostics data.

Google processes this data as a controller under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. In the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions, the game will ask for your consent through Google's consent form before showing personalised ads, and you can change it later at any time.

You can limit personalised ads on your phone: Android Settings → Google → Ads (delete or reset the advertising ID).

3. Purchases

The game does not include real-money purchases.

4. Children

Age of Cats is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided data, write to us and we will delete it.

5. Security and retention

Since your progress stays on your device, you control that data. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

6. Your rights

Because we do not store personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Regarding Google's advertising data, you can manage it in Google My Ad Center or exercise your rights (access, erasure, objection) with Google under the GDPR.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the game changes (for example, if it adds new features). We will publish the new version here with its date.

8. Contact
